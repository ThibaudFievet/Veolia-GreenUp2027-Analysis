      Section 07 · Verdict, Recommendation & Honest Limits Report pp. 36–41 · Falsifiable Advisory

      Verdict, Actionable Bridge & What We Could Not Establish

      Verdict: WTS strengthens economics (ESG neutral). Clean Earth strengthens the model ONLY under 3 verifiable conditions.
      1 · Water Technologies (WTS)

      Economic +, ESG Neutral

      Low execution risk (~€40m of €90m synergies achieved) and immediate net income lift, but zero incremental ESG impact and unverifiable ~11x multiple.

      2 · Clean Earth (Conditional)

      3 Conditions Required

      (1) Synergies ≥~$60m/yr by 2028 (55%–70% delivery); (2) €2bn+ disposals executed so leverage ≤3x in 2027; (3) ESG reported at constant perimeter.

      3 · Three US Tuck-Ins (€190m)

      Too Small & Opaque

      NEDT (€67m), Bio-Med (€33m), Ingenium (€90m) lack standalone revenue, EBITDA, and volumes: too little disclosed to alter the group verdict.

      Recommendation: Publish a 24-Month “Acquisition Impact Bridge”

      Cost & Who Bears It (Brief Requirement)

      Reporting effort (finance & ESG teams, data already tracked internally via synergy volumes & zone CO₂ budgets ) plus a communication cost for management (smaller headline KPI gains, possible pay-KPI re-basing). Investors, auditors & the post-2027 plan benefit.

      Falsifiability · What Would Change Our Mind?

      More negative: synergies <$60m/yr by 2028, >1 yr delay, or WACC >10%. More positive: physical proof of internalised disposal & shorter transport, or keeping Clean Earth out of waste KPIs until restated. Could we have reached the opposite conclusion? Yes: observations, not framing, decided every answer.
